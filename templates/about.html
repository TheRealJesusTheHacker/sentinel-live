About Sentinel Safeguard

A cybersecurity and IT company based in Kansas City, Missouri. President-led, appointment-only, and built on the same infrastructure it sells — this page states what the company is, who leads it, and what it does, plainly and without embellishment.

The company

Sentinel Safeguard LLC is a Missouri limited liability company, organized in January 2024 and based in Kansas City, Missouri. It serves the Kansas City metro area. The company works by appointment only — there are no walk-ins. Call, email, or send a message, and a time is set.

Sentinel's position is practical: security should make a business stronger, not slower. Findings come in plain language, fixes are prioritized by impact and effort, and testing is authorized and scoped in writing before it begins — authorized work only, every time.

Leadership

Sentinel Safeguard is president-led. The company is led by Skyler Paul Henderson, President & Lead Director of Operations. The person who scopes the work is the person who directs it — there is no hand-off to a sales layer or a rotating bench of account managers. He is currently completing CompTIA Security+.

From our own house

Sentinel holds its own house to the standard it recommends. The website you are reading, the operations platform behind it, and Sentinel's mail all run on infrastructure Sentinel operates itself. The mail system is hosted in-house, on Sentinel's own server — not rented from a platform — and the operations platform is not a public website at all: it sits behind an owner-controlled gate, separate from this site.

Every night, each of those systems is backed up. Every archive carries a fingerprint manifest of its contents, is encrypted as it is created — with the key that opens it held by the owner, off the server — and a copy is pulled off-site. Restores are drilled on a schedule and the results logged; a backup that has never been restored is treated here as a hope, not a backup.

Sentinel also publishes how to report a problem in its own systems: a responsible disclosure policy and a standing security contact, so a good-faith report reaches us and gets an answer. The platform's availability is reported on a public page fed by live checks, and the platform itself has been audited against the NIST SP 800-53 control families — with every fixed finding independently re-verified before it was called done.
