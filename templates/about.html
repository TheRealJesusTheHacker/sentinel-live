About Sentinel Safeguard

A cybersecurity and IT company based in Kansas City, Missouri. President-led, appointment-only, and built on the same infrastructure it sells — this page states what the company is, who leads it, and what it does, plainly and without embellishment.

The company

Sentinel Safeguard LLC is a Missouri limited liability company, organized in January 2024 and based in Kansas City, Missouri. It serves the Kansas City metro area. The company works by appointment only — there are no walk-ins. Call, email, or send a message, and a time is set.

Sentinel's position is practical: security should make a business stronger, not slower. Findings come in plain language, fixes are prioritized by impact and effort, and testing is authorized and scoped in writing before it begins — authorized work only, every time.

Leadership

Sentinel Safeguard is president-led. The company is led by Skyler Paul Henderson, President & Lead Director of Operations. The person who scopes the work is the person who directs it — there is no hand-off to a sales layer or a rotating bench of account managers. He is currently completing CompTIA Security+.

What we do

Current lines of work:

Cybersecurity services

Assessment and hardening of the systems a business actually depends on — devices, accounts, networks, and daily workflows.

IT services

Secure configuration, troubleshooting, and practical improvement of business technology, on-site and remote.

Penetration testing

Controlled, authorized testing that shows which weaknesses are actually exploitable — with evidence, business context, and a remediation path.

Digital forensics

Evidence-led examination of devices and data when something has gone wrong or needs to be understood.

Incident response

Structured help when a security event happens — contain, understand, and recover in the right order.

Security system installs

On-site installation of the physical and technical systems that protect a premises and the people in it.

Education

Straightforward security education for teams and individuals — what the risks are and what to do about them.

Planned — not yet offered

Private security guard services and private investigation services are planned for the future. They are not current offerings.
