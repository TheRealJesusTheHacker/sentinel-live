After the round

The tells, in one list

Who is really asking?

Read the sender's actual address, not the display name. A message can call itself anything; the address behind it is harder to dress up. On a phone, tap the sender to see it in full.

Where does the link go?

Hover on a computer, or press and hold on a phone, to see the real destination before you land there. One wrong letter in a familiar name is the whole trick.

What is the hurry?

Urgency is a tool. “Locked in one hour,” “final notice,” “act now” — real account problems survive the two minutes it takes to check through the front door.

Were you expecting it?

An invoice you never ordered, a delivery you never scheduled, a form you never asked for — surprise is a tell all by itself. Verify with the sender on a number you already have.
