Kansas City · IT Support

IT support in the Kansas City metro

Small businesses do not need an IT department. They need the technology they already own to work dependably — and one number to call when it does not. Sentinel provides day-to-day IT help for small businesses and homes across the Kansas City metro, on-site first, with remote help when it fits.

The everyday work, handled

New machines set up properly instead of out of the box. Accounts and email that the right people can reach and nobody else can. Updates applied and checked. Backups that are confirmed, not hoped for. A printer, a workstation, a network that keeps dropping — the unglamorous list that eats a small business's week is exactly the list this service exists to take off it.

Because Sentinel is a security company first, the everyday work is done the secure way by default: default passwords changed, access kept sensible, and settings documented — so today's quick fix does not become tomorrow's open door.

Who it is for

Small offices, service companies, and community organizations across the Kansas City metro that have outgrown fixing it themselves — and households whose home office has become the office.

What you walk away with

Technology that works, a record of what was done and how things are set up, and plain language throughout — no jargon billed by the hour, no mystery about your own systems.

How it runs

The company works by appointment only — there are no walk-ins. You reach out, the work is talked through and scoped in writing, and a time is set. How an engagement runs from first message to final walkthrough is stated plainly on How It Works, and the full Cybersecurity & IT service covers how support and security work fit together.

Something wrong right now — a locked account, a machine behaving strangely, data where it should not be? Do not wait on an appointment: the report page is open around the clock and reaches Sentinel directly.
