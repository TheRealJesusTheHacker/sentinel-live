Service 01

Penetration testing

A penetration test is a controlled attack on your own systems, carried out with your permission, to find out which weaknesses are actually exploitable before someone without permission does. Sentinel tests the systems your business depends on — networks, devices, accounts, and the web presence in front of them — and shows its work: evidence for every finding, not scanner noise.

Who it is for

Businesses that hold customer data, take payments, or would struggle to explain a breach to the people who trust them — and anyone who wants to know their real exposure instead of guessing at it.

What you walk away with

A findings report written in plain language: what was found, the evidence behind it, what it puts at risk, and the fixes in priority order — a report you act on, not a scare sheet. What held up is stated just as plainly.

Authorized work only: no system is ever tested without signed written authorization first, and the scope is agreed in writing before testing begins. That is not fine print — it is the professionalism.

Service 02

Cybersecurity & IT for small business

Most small businesses run on technology nobody has ever sat down and secured. Sentinel starts with an assessment of what you actually have — devices, accounts, network, and daily workflows — then hardens it: secure configuration, sensible access, updates and backups that are verified rather than assumed. After the hardening, ongoing protection keeps it that way, with on-site work first and remote help when it fits.

Who it is for

Small businesses and organizations across the Kansas City metro that have outgrown "a computer and a hope" — offices, service companies, and community organizations that need their technology dependable and their data protected, without hiring an IT department.

What you walk away with

Systems that are assessed, hardened, and documented — you know what you have, how it is protected, and who to call when something looks wrong. Plain language throughout; no jargon billed by the hour.

Service 03

Security installs

Camera and access-control systems, installed on-site and configured properly — by the company that also knows how these systems get attacked. An install is not finished when the picture appears on a screen: default passwords are gone, access is restricted to the people who should have it, recording and remote viewing are set up securely, and the whole system is checked the way an intruder would check it.

Who it is for

Businesses and property operators who need eyes on a premises and control over who gets in — and who want the system protecting them, not quietly exposing them.

What you walk away with

A working system you know how to use: cameras and access points placed with intent, hardened settings, and a walkthrough of how to view, manage, and maintain what was installed.

Service 04

Incident response & forensics

When something is wrong right now — a compromised account, a machine behaving strangely, data where it should not be — the order of operations matters. Sentinel helps you contain the problem first, then understand it: what happened, what was touched, and what it means. Where evidence matters, it is handled carefully and examined in a way that preserves what it can prove.

Who it is for

Anyone staring at a suspected breach, a locked account, or a device they no longer trust — and anyone who needs a careful, evidence-led answer to "what actually happened here?"

What you walk away with

A contained problem and a straight account of it: what was found, what was affected, and the steps that keep it from happening again.

Something wrong right now? The report page is open around the clock — a report reaches Sentinel directly and starts the conversation.
