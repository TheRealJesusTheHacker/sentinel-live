      How this is set: the level is computed from three public inputs, and nothing else. First, how many vulnerabilities CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog in the last 7 days (18 or more earns 3 points, 10–17 earns 2, 4–9 earns 1). Second, how many Threat Watch and Patch Alert items Sentinel News published in the last 7 days (45 or more earns 2 points, 25–44 earns 1). Third, how many attacking sources the DShield sensor network counted today (260,000 or more earns 2 points, 180,000 or more earns 1). The points add to a score of 0–7: 0–1 is level 1 ROUTINE, 2–3 is level 2 GUARDED, 4–5 is level 3 ELEVATED, 6 is level 4 HIGH, and 7 is level 5 SEVERE. There is no hand-tuning — the number is the number, and the inputs below are the exact values it came from.
