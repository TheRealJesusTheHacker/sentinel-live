      Questions, answered straight.

      What happens on the first call?

      We talk through your operation, your priorities, and what is getting in the way — then set a clear scope before anything else happens. You will know what we are looking at, what we found, and what comes next. From there we inspect and test, then help you fix things in the right order.

      Do you work on-site or remote?

      Both. Sentinel Safeguard is Kansas City–based, and on-site work leads: hands-on assessments of entry points, cameras, devices, networks, and daily workflows. Remote work stays in the mix when it fits the job.

      Is testing authorized and scoped?

      Yes — authorized work only, every time. Penetration testing is controlled and scoped in writing before testing begins, and you get evidence, business context, and a remediation path — not a noisy list of scanner output.

      We're a small team. Is this built for us?

      That is exactly who Sentinel is built for: local businesses, independent operators, startups, and creative or technical shops that need strong security without adding a department to manage it. Findings come in plain language, and fixes are prioritized by impact and effort.

      Can you recover data — or make sure it's gone?

      Both. We recover what can be recovered, and when sensitive data needs to stay gone, we handle secure destruction and drive wiping. We help you choose the right method for the device and the risk, and document the result.
